SOURCE & STRATEGY

Use the Nearest Copies First.

First reserve existing copies at the target rarity, then use copies one tier below, and continue downward only as needed. Execute the resulting merges from low to high. This minimizes merge fees for a fixed target with the entered inventory; it leaves unrelated copies alone. All copies above the target remain unused. This is a target-limited plan, not the game’s “Forge All” cascade, which merges every available tier.

Any remaining shortage is expressed as additional Common copies of this same item. This defines one deterministic completion route. Obtaining higher-rarity copies instead can reduce both the copy requirement and the gold bill. The displayed fees exclude buying or acquiring those missing copies. No drop rarity, drop time or acquisition outcome is guaranteed.

The source requires three copies of the same item and rarity per merge, producing one copy of the next rarity. The cap is Primordial, tier 21. Each fee is round(base × materialTier^1.8 / 100) × 100, rounded before multiplying by the number of merges. Material tier is separate from rarity; the catalog uses the game’s exact-name and prefix rules.

Only source-eligible equipment and gathering tools appear. Legendary-flagged items, excluded items, ordinary ammunition, and items without a forge material tier are omitted. Sigils and runes follow their source eligibility. “Legendary” rarity is still a valid target for eligible items. Equipped copies are unavailable until unequipped.

Stats reuse isolated source preview formulas: ordinary stats floor the rarity product; special bonuses use their own scaling; tool speed scales at one quarter of the rarity-multiplier increase. Requirements use exact integer arithmetic, including large totals. Inputs accept up to 30 digits. Your inputs stay in this page and are not uploaded or saved.
