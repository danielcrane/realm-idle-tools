Built from the Game’s Rules

This simulator uses 396 equipment definitions, all 21 rarity tiers, 80 monsters, blessings, pets, potions, and 695 packaged images from version 4.1.3.8. Active combat runs the original fightTick with presentation and real-time timers replaced.

Damage and Timing

Gear rounding and multiplier order, minimum hits, monster defense, lifesteal, regeneration, auto-eat safety checks, and killing-hit retaliation rules come from the source. An attack takes 600 ms. Between enemies there is an 800 ms death delay, plus a random 1–2 second walk scaled by your walk bonuses. Removing all walking still leaves the death delay. The first active attack starts at 650 ms.

Three Distinct Modes

Active uses the original combat tick and random walks. Background return uses mean walking time carried as fractional 600 ms ticks, and deducts 600 ms plus accumulated walking time from combat potions before damage. Cold offline starts at full HP, deducts 600 ms before damage and mean walking time after kills, and adds mean walk time directly. Away modes preserve the bundled loop’s boundary behavior, cached stats, and ammo order; they are not exact forecasts of device-specific foreground/background transitions.

Reading the Results

The damage card describes your starting bonuses. Session results include buff expiry, supply exhaustion, and death. Kills/hour is total kills divided by the entire requested session, including time after combat stops. The 5th–95th percentile range describes variation between simulated sessions. The mean’s 95% confidence interval measures sampling error, not model error. Zero observed deaths does not prove a setup is safe.

Controlled Assumptions

  • Choose season and weather manually; their bonuses apply throughout the simulation. Candy strength is explicit (normally 50% outside its event); active candy expires at the selected time. Away modes use the session-weighted bonus.
  • By default, levels and bestiary milestones stay fixed for fair loadout comparisons. Optional active progression starts with profile XP where available; modified levels start at their XP thresholds and applies XP, defense HP increases, bestiary milestones, and first boss clears.
  • Loot is an expected yield, conditional on each simulated kill. Loot is not fed back into supplies, equipment, or museum collections. Pet drops, event currencies, rarity-roll outcomes, quests, world bosses, and skill bosses are outside this model.
  • The selected ammo/spell is already equipped. The ammo inventory contains spares. Infinite runes need no stock. The original game’s active and offline ammo replacement differences are preserved.
  • Manual museum fields are final bonuses from the game. Collection mode derives bonuses from a recorded collection and your equipped gear; it requires the Ancient Dungeon boss clear. Equipment is available for hypothetical planning regardless of ownership and unlock costs.
  • No device lag, server changes, AFK entitlement caps, portal closures, or manual player actions are modeled. Sessions start at a fresh monster with auto-fight enabled. Any boss in the normal monster tables is supported.

Profiles and baseline comparisons are saved in this browser. Exported JSON contains only this simulator’s settings. Nothing is uploaded.
