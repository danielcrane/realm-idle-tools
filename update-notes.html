Update Notes

What changed, and how it works in play.

11 October 2026 · Latest Release

4.1.3.9

More Rare Gathering Finds

Bird Nests, Gem Bags and Underwater Chests use a new resource-level curve. At the top resource, unboosted rates rise from 40 to 70 nests/hour, 30 to 52.5 bags/hour, and 24 to 42 chests/hour. Pet and armor bonuses still multiply these rates. Faster actions do not directly increase finds per hour.

This is a curved adjustment, not a flat 75% increase across all resources. At level 100 Woodcutting, the unboosted estimate is about 52.37 nests/hour, up from 35.31.

Rare Gathering Formula and Node Rates

Previous resource factor (linear):

0.35 + 0.65 * (L / Lmax)

4.1.3.9 resource factor (exponential):

0.35 * 5^(L / Lmax)

Full hourly-rate formula:

Finds per hour = B * 0.35 * 5^(L / Lmax)
                * (1 + P) * (1 + G)

L is the node's required level, not your character level. Lmax is the highest node requirement: 122 for Woodcutting and Mining, 128 for Fishing. B is 40 for Bird Nests, 30 for Gem Bags, and 24 for Underwater Chests. P is the matching pet treasure-find bonus (1 for +100%); G is the sum of matching armor treasure-find bonuses, expressed as a decimal.

These tables show expected finds per productive gathering hour with no pet or armor bonuses, rounded to two decimals. They are averages, not guaranteed hourly counts. For example, a matching +100% pet doubles every displayed rate; armor bonuses multiply that result. Gathering speed does not directly multiply these rates.

Gathering Chest Chances Reach 90%

Each gathering-boss chest size now reaches a 90% chance at the top of its damage band, up from 75%. On a miss, every smaller size is tried in descending order at 90% each. There is still at most one chest per attempt.

At the top of the largest band, the outcomes are 90% large, 9% medium, 0.9% small and 0.1% no chest. The chance for a newly entered size still starts at 5%.

Gathering Boss Damage Thresholds

The same damage bands apply to Elder Treant, Kraken and Stone Golem. D is damage in one attempt; p is the desired primary chest chance as a decimal (0.85 means 85%). L and H are the lower and upper damage limits of the selected chest band.

p = 0.05 + 0.85 * clamp((D - L) / (H - L), 0, 1)
D = L + (H - L) * (p - 0.05) / 0.85

Round damage up to the next whole point. At zero damage, the chance is zero. Minor uses L = 0, H = 250,000; regular uses L = 250,000, H = 1,500,000; Grand uses L = 1,500,000, H = 2,500,000.

Ashlyn's Chest Chance Keeps Climbing

The chance remains 75% at 1.5 million damage per attempt, then rises linearly to 90% at 3 million. For example, 2 million damage gives 80% and 2.5 million gives 85%. Damage above 3 million does not increase this chance further.

Nine New Zone Quests

Questlines → General now groups Zone Quests by zone. Nine new quests bring each zone to two quests, including boss objectives and additional gold and equipment rewards.

Delete Account

The Account tab now offers permanent account deletion, including progress and purchases. It requires typing DELETE twice. Reset Progress remains a different operation.

Food Notifications

A new Food notification toggle in Account is enabled by default. The game estimates when food will run out during a fight and schedules a warning a few minutes beforehand. Notification permission is required; the estimate is not a guarantee.

Community Center Refreshes

Community Center bonuses refresh when opening the game, then every 30 minutes.

Keep Individual Ammo Rarities

Arrows and spells can now be locked by rarity using the lock on each rarity row, so selected tiers can be reserved while others remain available.

Full Hunter's Lodge Count

Hunter's Lodge displays the full Total Vanquished count.

Gathering Potion Pause Correction

The release fixes gathering potions counting down in the background while another skill is active.

Offline Drop Quantities

Offline rewards with quantity ranges now use their fractional average rather than rounding that average down first. A 1–2 drop therefore averages 1.5 items per successful drop, with final quantities rounded probabilistically.

Potion Replacement Confirmation

The release fixes some Replace Potion confirmation prompts closing immediately.

Additional Release Changes

Summoning Pauses Production Potions

Rune and quest-relic summoning is excluded from Production Potion consumption. Summons retain their fixed 30-second duration.

Offline Combat Recalculates at Level-Ups

Offline and background combat recalculate player stats when combat skills gain levels, so later kills use the updated stats.

Active-Time Accounting

Offline activity totals and skilling-pet rolls now use time actually spent working. Production that runs out of materials no longer counts the entire absence as productive time.

Gathering Pet Rewards

Woodcutting double-resource and triple-log effects can both apply. Offline extra-resource rolls use probabilistic rounding, and gathering XP potion bonuses are included in extra-resource XP.

Cloud Tool Slots

Cloud loading preserves empty tool slots. Community Center state is also merged in additional cloud-recovery paths.

Forge and Inventory Handling

Forge One uses its dedicated plan calculation. Frozen Heart Pendant is treated as an all-style item in equipment presentation. Offline combat stopping no longer clears the Auto Fight preference.

4 October 2026

4.1.3.8

The five changes in this release, with explanations of how they work in play.

Potions Now Use Active Time

Potions last for a set amount of active time instead of a fixed number of attacks or actions. Their bonus strength stays the same, but faster attacks, gathering or production can now fit more boosted actions into one dose.

Active Time per Dose
Tier or PrefixDuration
Minor4 minutes
Base and other prefixes, including Rose and Haunted5 minutes
Greater6 minutes
Supreme, Frost and Easter7 minutes
Abyssal8 minutes
Aeon10 minutes

The timer follows the activity the potion boosts, both online and offline:

  • Combat potions count down during combat, including walking and death delays between enemies in an ongoing fight. A slower kill cycle therefore spends more potion time per kill.
  • Gathering speed and gathering XP potions count down during Woodcutting, Mining and Fishing, as well as gathering-boss attempts.
  • Production potions count down during Cooking, Smithing, Crafting, Alchemy and Arcane Arts. Divinity and Thieving are outside this group.

Under the activity rules, switching to an unrelated activity pauses the potion. For example, using two minutes of a Supreme combat potion leaves five minutes for later combat. Candies and pet whistles still use elapsed real time, so their expiry does not pause when you change activities.

Drinking the same potion again adds its duration to the time remaining: two fresh Supreme doses give 14 minutes. A stronger potion for the same stat replaces the weaker effect after a warning and discards its remaining time; a weaker potion is rejected. Potions for different stats can run together.

Effects already running when you update keep the same proportion of a dose. Half of an old Supreme potion becomes 3 minutes 30 seconds; a quarter becomes 1 minute 45 seconds. Offline gathering separates boosted actions from actions after expiry. Production similarly separates boosted and normal batches and deducts time spent producing, stopping when materials run out. Partial actions at an expiry boundary are rounded rather than receiving a proportional bonus.

Production Potions Need One Empty Bird Nest

Every tier of Production Potion now requires one Empty Bird Nest per recipe. Opening a Bird Nest gives one Empty Bird Nest to use in Alchemy. The change reduces the nest requirement of recipes that previously needed more; the other recipe ingredients are still required.

Bird Nests still arrive at the existing time-based Woodcutting rate. That rate depends on the tree's required level and your pet and equipment treasure bonuses. Faster tools and gathering-speed potions do not directly multiply nest income.

For example, a level-100 tree gives about 35.31 nests per hour without treasure bonuses, or 70.62 with the Elder Treant pet's double-nest bonus before equipment bonuses. The recipe change lets the same nest supply cover more brewing where the old recipe cost was higher.

Haunted Hollow Walking Is Faster After the Wraith Clear

Defeating the Haunted Relic Wraith unlocks 10 percentage points of walking reduction in Haunted Hollow. The bonus appears on the zone's map card and adds to Hunter's Lodge and pet walking reductions, up to a total of 100%.

It reduces the travel part of the delay between enemies. The fixed 0.8-second post-kill delay remains, and attack time is unchanged. Average time between a kill and the next fight is:

0.8 + 1.5 × (1 − total walking reduction) seconds, using the reduction as a fraction: 10% is 0.10.

With no other walking bonuses, the average delay falls from 2.30 to 2.15 seconds, saving 0.15 seconds per kill. If Lodge and pet bonuses already give 95% reduction, only five of the new ten percentage points fit below the cap, saving 0.075 seconds.

The gain matters most when fights are short. For a one-hit kill taking 0.6 seconds, the full average cycle falls from 2.90 to 2.75 seconds, increasing the theoretical rate from about 1,241 to 1,309 kills per hour. Longer fights receive the same travel saving but a smaller percentage increase in kills per hour.

Pet Whistle Is Now Called Combat Pet Whistle

The new name makes the whistle's purpose clearer: Combat Pet Whistle boosts boss and monster pet catch rates, while Skilling Pet Whistle boosts skilling pet catch rates. The rename does not change which pets each whistle affects.

Existing whistles keep working under the new display name. The one-day and seven-day variants keep their durations, and using another of the same whistle extends its active timer. Both kinds use real-time expiry, including time spent away or doing another activity; they do not use the new active-time potion rules.

Empty Equipment Slots Stay Empty on Cloud Load

Loading a cloud save now preserves intentionally empty equipment slots. Previously, loading with an empty slot could introduce default Copper equipment, causing a Copper Helm to appear or a Copper Shield to land in the inventory.

The load now restores the equipment recorded in the cloud save while leaving empty slots empty. For example, if you save with your helm removed, loading that save should leave the helm slot empty instead of supplying a starter Copper Helm.

27 September 2026

4.1.3.6

17 changes from the game's changelog, with additional explanations where useful.

Bows and Crossbows Raise Minimum Damage

Bows and crossbows raise your minimum hit like one-hand swords: +2% on the Short Bow up to +12% on the Aeonscale Bow, Ashlyn's Longbow and Wraithpiercer, on top of the level floor, so lifesteal heals more evenly.

The weapon bonus adds to the minimum damage already provided by your skill level. Raising that floor makes low rolls less severe and gives lifesteal a steadier amount of damage to heal from.

Auto-Eat Responds to Lethal Hits

Auto-eat also eats when the monster's next hit could kill you, whatever your threshold, online and offline.

Your chosen threshold still governs normal eating. This additional check can trigger food earlier when waiting for the threshold would leave you vulnerable to the next hit.

Candy Strength Follows the Halloween Season

Candies are full strength during Halloween and half strength after it ends, for running timers and candies still in the bag.

The seasonal reduction applies to the effect's strength, including candy already active when the event ends. Candy kept in your inventory also uses the reduced strength when consumed after the event.

Gathering Candies Apply to Gathering Bosses

Gathering candies now work on gathering bosses too.

Pokoli's Extra Candy Appears in Session Reports

Pokoli's extra candy shows as Items Found by Pokoli in the session trackers and Welcome Back, like skilling pets.

Haunted Bosses Drop Frozen Core

The Haunted Relic Wraith and the Haunted Lich drop Frozen Core.

Haunted Portal Availability Follows the Event

The Haunted Portal comes off the Event Shop when candy stops dropping on November 1, since the Hollow closes then. It returns next Halloween, and a portal you own stays yours.

Owning the portal is permanent, but access to the Hollow remains seasonal. Keeping a portal does not keep the zone open after the event closes.

Chocolate Bars Are Reserved for Valentine's Day

Chocolate Bars are no longer food. Keep them for the Valentine's Day Event Shop in February.

Equipped Items Show Only Worn Gear

Equipped in the Items tab shows only what you wear. Spare copies of that gear stay in their Melee, Ranged or Magic section.

Consumables Use Compact Rows and Bulk Actions

Items tab: potions, candies, pets, tomes and the other non-gear sections are compact rows, and Event Consumables have Use 1x, 10x and 100x like potions.

Locks Protect Food, Arrows and Spells

Items tab: tap the lock on a food, arrow or spell to keep it. Auto-eat, ammo switching and auto-equip skip what you keep, online and offline.

Locking an item reserves it from the listed automatic systems. The protection applies during active play and offline calculations.

Weapons Are Grouped by Handedness

Weapon picker: One-Handed and Two-Handed tabs when you own both.

Pet Colours Share a Picker Row

Pet picker: one row per pet with the other colours you own next to it, tap a colour to wear it. Pet lore shows in the Item Index.

Halloween Help Explains the Event

How it works added to the Halloween map card and the Event Shop: the dates, the Haunted Hollow, and what happens to candies after the event.

Magic and Ranged Pages Explain Ammo Sources

Magic and Ranged skill pages say where spells and arrows come from.

Themed App Icons Display Correctly

Fixed the app icon showing as a plain black or white shape on phones with themed icons.

Matching Rings Unequip from the Correct Slot

Fixed Unequip on the second of two matching rings removing the first one.

When both ring slots hold matching rings, choosing Unequip on the second slot now removes that slot's ring.
