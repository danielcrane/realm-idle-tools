    ABOUT THE MODEL

    Training Rules from the Bundled Game.

    The web calculator now uses the extracted Realm Idle 4.1.3.8 action tables, XP curve, blessings, pets, equipment, and potion catalogs. Rune unlock records are excluded because they are not timed training actions. Combat-only and drop-only bonuses are excluded from the selectors.

    The target plan uses offline rules and solves for the time required to reach your target. Selected season and weather bonuses apply throughout the plan. Candy effects account for duration and event dates. Potions run out after their recorded active minutes; material limits can prevent reaching the target. Enabled upgrades assume immediate player intervention.

    XP is the awarded amount before the game’s level-130 storage clamp. Level progress stops at 130.
